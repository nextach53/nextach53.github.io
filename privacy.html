Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · applies to NEXTACH BOOK, FEE and DORMA (Windows) and NEXTACH PUNCH (Android)

In short

  • Everything you enter stays on your own phone or computer. NEXTACH does not run servers that receive it.
  • Google Drive backup is optional. The app can reach only the backup files it creates in your own Drive.
  • WhatsApp messages are optional. If the Admin links a WhatsApp number, the app sends only the messages described below.
  • We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

1. Who we are

NEXTACH develops business software: NEXTACH BOOK (accounts & inventory), FEE (coaching institutes) and DORMA (hostels & PGs) for Windows, and NEXTACH PUNCH, an attendance and payroll app for Android. Sections 2, 3 and 9 describe PUNCH; section 10 describes the Windows products. Contact: nextach53@gmail.com.

2. Data the app keeps on your device

The business that installs NEXTACH PUNCH (the "Admin") enters and controls this data. It is stored only in the app's private storage on that device:

  • Company profile and logo, attendance month and settings.
  • Employee details entered by the Admin: name, code, department, designation, phone number, joining date, shift, leave entitlements, photo and, if the Admin adds them, the Aadhaar number and Aadhaar card pictures. Reports show only the last 4 digits of the Aadhaar number.
  • Face templates: when an employee's face is enrolled, the app stores numbers that describe the face (not a picture) so it can recognise the employee at the attendance kiosk. Face matching runs entirely on the device.
  • Attendance punches, corrections with their reasons, leaves and reports.
  • App users (Admin and users), their PINs (stored only as a secure one-way hash) and an activity log.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan faces and ID card QR codes for attendance, and to take employee photos. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not uploaded.
  • Fingerprint / biometrics: to unlock the app login using Android's own fingerprint prompt. The app never receives or stores fingerprints.
  • Internet: only for Google Drive backup and, if the Admin links WhatsApp, for sending WhatsApp messages.

4. Google Drive backup (optional)

This applies to NEXTACH PUNCH on Android and to NEXTACH BOOK, FEE and DORMA on Windows. If the Admin clicks "Connect Google Drive", Google asks the Admin to choose a Google account and allow access. The software asks only for the permission drive.file, which means it can see and manage only the files it creates itself: backups in the folders NEXTACH_PUNCH_Backup, NEXTACH_BOOK_Backup, NEXTACH_FEE_Backup or NEXTACH_DORMA_Backup. It cannot see any other files in that Google Drive. It uses this access only to upload, list, download (for restore) and remove old backup files, and it reads the account's e-mail address to show which account is connected.

Backups go directly from the customer's own phone or computer to the Admin's own Google Drive. NEXTACH does not receive them. On Windows the Google permission is stored on that computer, encrypted for its Windows user. The Admin can disconnect at any time in the software, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

NEXTACH's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google APIs is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to anyone.

5. Automatic WhatsApp messages (optional)

In WhatsApp Setup the Admin can link the company's own WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code, as with WhatsApp Web. The link is stored on the device. Depending on what the Admin switches on, the app then sends through that number:

  • the daily and monthly attendance reports, to the report numbers set by the Admin. These can contain employee names and attendance details;
  • a confirmation to an employee after each punch, and an "attendance not marked" reminder, to the mobile number in the Employee Master. These contain the employee's name, the time and the shift.

These messages travel through WhatsApp, a service of Meta, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. NEXTACH does not receive them. The Admin is responsible for informing employees and can switch the messages off or unlink the number at any time. A list of sent messages is kept on the device.

6. Data that leaves the device only when you choose

  • Licence activation: the Admin creates a request file and sends it to their NEXTACH provider. It contains the company name and contact details, the number of users and employees, and a device code made from the phone's identifiers by a one-way hash. It is used only to issue and manage the licence.
  • Reports and backups shared by you: PDF, Excel, WhatsApp messages or backup files go only where you send them, using the apps you choose.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the device until the Admin deletes it. In Company Profile the Admin can choose to keep only the last few years of data; older data is removed automatically after a backup is made. Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device. Backups in Google Drive stay until the Admin deletes the backup folder (NEXTACH_PUNCH_Backup, NEXTACH_BOOK_Backup, NEXTACH_FEE_Backup or NEXTACH_DORMA_Backup). Employees who want their data corrected or removed should contact their employer (the Admin), who controls it.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android. App logins use PINs or the phone's fingerprint unlock, and each user has only the rights the Admin gives.

9. Children

NEXTACH PUNCH is a business tool for employers and is not directed at children.

10. NEXTACH BOOK, FEE and DORMA (Windows)

  • Where data is kept: on the customer's own head-office computer. Branches and other computers open it through a web browser; nothing is stored on NEXTACH servers.
  • Access over the Internet (optional): if the customer turns on Remote access, branches reach the head-office computer through a secure link that the customer sets up (for example a Cloudflare Tunnel on the customer's own account). NEXTACH does not receive this traffic.
  • WhatsApp (optional): as in section 5, bills, receipts, reminders and reports are sent through the number the Admin links, to the customer, student, resident or report numbers entered in the software.
  • Currency rates (NEXTACH BOOK): once a day the software reads public exchange rates from a rate service on the Internet. Only the request for the day's rates is sent - no business data.
  • Licence activation: the request file contains the business name and contact details, the numbers of branches, users and locations requested, and a computer code made from the computer's identifiers by a one-way hash.

11. Changes

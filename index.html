Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Download the setup and install it - the trial starts right away with 1 branch, 1 Admin and 2 Users. Activate only when you are happy with it.

Is there a monthly fee? What does it cost?

No monthly fee. The software is a one-time, lifetime purchase: BOOK ₹8,000, FEE ₹6,000, DORMA ₹6,000 per computer and PUNCH ₹5,200 per phone. The company and user setup is yearly - ₹1,499 for 1 company, 1 Admin and 2 users (PUNCH: and 5 employees) (extra user ₹249, branch ₹999). Taxes extra. WhatsApp us for today's offer.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Daily billing, stock and accounts work on your own computer without internet. Internet is only needed for WhatsApp sending, Google Drive backup, exchange rates and branches working over the Internet.

Where is my data kept? Is it safe?

On your own computer or phone - not on our servers. Backups go to your PC and, if you like, your own Google Drive.

Will an update delete my data?

Never. Updates keep all your data, and a backup is taken before every update.

Does BOOK support GST, e-Invoice and e-Way Bill?

Yes - GST bills, e-Invoice and e-Way Bill details, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, CMP-08 and GSTR-2B matching. For UAE, Kenya, Tanzania and Kuwait each branch follows its own VAT rules and currency.

What is Dual Book?

NEXTACH BOOK keeps two books - A and C - in one software. Every entry is marked A or C, and reports show All, A or C. Both books open only with your Full Login Code; other users see A.

Can several people and branches use it?

Yes. Add users with their own rights, and run branches in other cities or countries from one head-office PC, 24×7, over the Internet.

How do I activate my licence?

In the software open Activation → Create Request File, send it to us on WhatsApp and import the licence file we send back. See the 3 steps.

Which computer or phone do I need?

BOOK, FEE and DORMA: Windows 10 or 11. PUNCH: an Android phone or tablet with Android 9 or newer.

For more information and activation, WhatsApp us - we usually reply the same day.
